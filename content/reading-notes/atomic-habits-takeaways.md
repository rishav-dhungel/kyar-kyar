## The Four Laws of Behavior Change

To build a good habit:
1. **Make it Obvious** (Cue)
2. **Make it Attractive** (Craving)
3. **Make it Easy** (Response)
4. **Make it Satisfying** (Reward)

> [!quote] Key Quote
> "You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems."

## Core Takeaways

### 1. Identity-Based Habits
The most effective way to change your habits is not to focus on what you want to achieve, but on **who you wish to become**:
- Instead of "I want to run a marathon", adopt "I am a runner."
- Instead of "I want to publish a blog", adopt "I am a thinker who journals every morning in [[journal/2026-09-29-morning-reflections|my journal]]."

### 2. Environment Design
Environment is the invisible hand that shapes human behavior. Make positive cues obvious and negative cues invisible:
- Leave water bottles on your desk
- Keep a clean desk with just a notebook or Markdown editor open
