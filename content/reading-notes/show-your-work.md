# Book Notes: Show Your Work! by Austin Kleon 🎨

Austin Kleon makes the case that you don't need to be a "genius" to share your ideas online. Instead, be an amateur who is simply willing to show the process.

> [!tip] The Scenius Concept
> Great ideas rarely come from lone geniuses in isolated towers. They emerge from "scenius" — a community of people learning, sharing, and building together.

## 3 Big Ideas
- **Share something small every day**: A quote, a rough draft, a mistake you learned from.
- **Become a documentarian of what you do**: Don't wait until the project is finished.
- **Teach what you know**: As soon as you learn something, explain it to someone one step behind you.

This is exactly why public [[thoughts/digital-gardening-vs-chronological-blogs|digital gardens]] are so powerful: they lower the bar for sharing incomplete, honest thoughts.
