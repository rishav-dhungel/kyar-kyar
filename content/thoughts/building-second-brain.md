# Building a Second Brain with Plain Text 🧠

The trap of Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) is spending 90% of your time tweaking plugins, color-coding tags, and rearranging folders, and only 10% of your time actually writing and thinking.

The antidote is radical simplicity: **Plain Markdown files, readable by any text editor, linked together with wikilinks.**

> [!warning] Beware the Collector's Fallacy
> Saving an article to a folder is not the same as understanding it. A note is only valuable if you rewrite the concept in your own voice and connect it to at least one existing note.

## The 4 Steps of Progressive Summarization
1. **Raw Capture**: Save key excerpts into a new note.
2. **Bold Passages**: Highlight only the core 20% of ideas.
3. **Executive Summary**: Write a short paragraph in your own words at the top.
4. **Link to Context**: Link using \`[[Note Name]]\` to notes like [[thoughts/digital-gardening-vs-chronological-blogs|Digital Gardening]].
