- 🏃 **Movement**: Outdoor walks and brewing manual pour-over coffee.

---

## Why I Built This Garden
I wanted a personal blog that felt like a quiet notebook rather than a noisy feed. No comments sections, no popups, no tracking cookies, and no complex CMS dashboard to log into. 

Just a directory of text files that I edit in my favorite text editor, pushed to GitHub and hosted for free on a worldwide edge CDN.
